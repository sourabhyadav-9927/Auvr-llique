Ask Us on WhatsApp ↗

Do I need to book in advance?

Yes, we strongly recommend reserving your appointment at least 2–3 days in advance (and 2–3 weeks for weekend or bridal dates) to guarantee your desired time slot.

How should I prepare for a hair color service?

We recommend washing your hair 24–48 hours prior to your color session. Clean, dry hair without heavy dry shampoo or styling waxes allows our formulas to deposit with maximum precision.

Can services be customized to my specific needs?

Always. Every treatment starts with an in-depth conversation. We frequently tailor mask formulas, balayage techniques, and skincare actives specifically to your goals.

What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?

We understand plans change. We appreciate at least 24 hours' notice if you need to reschedule so we can offer the appointment time to another guest on our waiting list.
